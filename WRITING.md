# Writing for System One Bench

Apply the creator's Unslop editing rules to original public copy. State the task,
mechanism or measured result. Replace a claim about how useful something feels with
what a reader can do or verify.

Use plain words and active sentences. Name the primary source. Preserve the baseline,
sample size, metric definition and strongest limitation beside a result. Distinguish
our interpretation from the author's measurement.

Use sentence case headings, straight quotes and little bold. Avoid em dashes,
parenthetical asides, rhetorical fragments, abstract technology metaphors and vague
attribution. Do not force a list into three items. Code syntax, source titles and
verbatim identifiers retain their required punctuation.

Edit catalog.json and guides.json, then regenerate. Review the generated pages for
sentences that could describe any product. Replace them with a specific fact or remove
them. Writing changes must not alter an immutable raw benchmark result.
